# Walmart Sales & Space Performance

Business analysis of Walmart sales, department share, and sales per square meter using Google Sheets to support inventory, space, and budget decisions.

## Business question

Which departments generate the strongest sales performance relative to their space allocation, and where should management focus inventory, floor-space, and budget decisions?

## Approach

The analysis integrated Walmart sales, store, and department data and focused on a small set of decision-oriented KPIs:

- Sales by department
- Department share of total sales
- Sales per square meter
- Relative department performance

The work was completed in Google Sheets using data integration, pivot tables, KPI development, and an executive summary view.

## Key finding

Three departments accounted for **36.43% of total sales**, indicating a meaningful concentration of commercial performance within a limited part of the department mix.

## Business recommendations

- Prioritize high-performing departments when reviewing inventory allocation.
- Use sales per square meter alongside total sales to evaluate space efficiency.
- Review space and budget allocation by department rather than relying on sales volume alone.
- Monitor department share over time to identify shifts in commercial performance.
